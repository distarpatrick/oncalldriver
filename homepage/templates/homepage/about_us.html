The Beginning

OCD LTD was founded with a clear commitment: to provide safe, reliable, and affordable car rental services combined with specialized training for new drivers. We saw a gap in the market — quality vehicles alone were not enough. Drivers needed support too.

Recognizing the Challenge

New drivers in Rwanda face real challenges on the road. We recognized this early and made driver education a cornerstone of our service — not just renting cars but supporting beginners in gaining real confidence behind the wheel.

Growth & Trust

Over the years we have grown into a trusted provider known for professionalism and personalized service. Hundreds of satisfied clients across Rwanda are a testament to our dedication to doing things right, every single time.

Today & Beyond

Today, On Call Driver LTD stands as a comprehensive platform combining vehicle rental, driver training, and full purchase support — proudly built for Rwandan roads, communities, and the people who drive on them every day.
