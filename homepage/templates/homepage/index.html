How it works

From browsing to driving away — four clear steps, with our team on call the whole way.

Find your car

Compare verified cars by price, fuel, transmission, mileage and location.

Book a visit

Pick a date and time to inspect the car in person with a sales agent.

Pay securely

Reserve with 50% or pay in full via MTN MoMo, Airtel Money or bank transfer.

Drive away

Get your invoice instantly and request an on-call driver for delivery.

Payments

Pay your way

Every payment gets a unique reference, is verified before your booking is confirmed, and comes with a downloadable invoice.

MTN

MTN MoMo

Approve a payment prompt on your phone with your MoMo PIN, or pay with our MoMoPay code.

  • Numbers starting 078 / 079
  • Instant confirmation
airtel

Airtel Money

Receive a USSD prompt on your Airtel line and confirm with your PIN in seconds.

  • Numbers starting 072 / 073
  • No extra app needed

Bank transfer

Transfer or deposit at your bank using your payment reference, then upload the slip.

  • Ideal for full payments
  • Verified by our finance team
