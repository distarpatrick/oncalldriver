FAQ

Common Questions

Everything you need to know before making your first booking or consignment.

How do I book a car?
Browse our listings, select a car you like, and click "Book Now". Fill in your details, choose full or half payment, select your preferred payment method (MTN MoMo, Airtel Money, bank transfer, or cash), and submit. Your booking number is issued instantly and our team follows up within hours.
What is the scheduling fee?
A small scheduling fee (typically {{ site.default_scheduling_fee|default:"5,000" }} RWF, though this varies per listing) is charged to confirm a physical car visit. This fee reserves the visit slot and is paid before a sales agent confirms your appointment. The exact fee is shown on each listing page.
Can I pay in instalments?
Yes. We offer a 50% deposit option on all cars. Pay half to secure the car and remove it from listings, then clear the remaining balance before final collection. Both payments are tracked and confirmed by our accountant team.
How does car consignment work?
Register an account and contact our team. Our marketers photograph your car, verify ownership documents, create a professional listing, and handle the entire sale including visits and payment collection. We charge a {{ site.commission_rate|default:"5" }}% commission only when the car sells — you pay nothing upfront.
How long does a booking take to confirm?
Bookings are typically confirmed within 1–2 business hours after payment is submitted. Your accountant will verify the payment and update your booking status. You'll see the change reflected in your profile under "My Bookings".
Can I cancel a booking or visit?
Visit schedules can be deleted directly from your profile. For booking cancellations, please contact our support team through the ticket system. Refund eligibility depends on the booking status and payment stage — our team will guide you through the process.
Which provinces do you cover?
We list cars from all five provinces of Rwanda: Kigali City, Northern Province, Southern Province, Eastern Province, and Western Province. Use the province filter on the car listing page to find vehicles near you.
Is my payment information secure?
Yes. All payments go through verified channels (MTN MoMo, Airtel Money, official bank transfer, or in-person cash). No card details are stored on our platform. Every transaction is reviewed by our accountant team before any booking is confirmed.
